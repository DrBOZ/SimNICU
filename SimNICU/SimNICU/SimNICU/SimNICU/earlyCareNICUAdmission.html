• Weigh in plastic bag
  • Do not remove forceps
  • Do not remove plastic bag until humidity and incubator temp correct
  • Temp should be > 36ºC
  • HR, RR, SaO2
  • Allow nurses time to admit
  • Do not waste time inserting peripheral cannula
  • UAC / UVC immediately after admission
    (target 30 min after admission)
  • 60 ml/kg/day 10% dextrose or starter Parenteral Nutrition
  • Give antibiotics
  • CXR / AXR immediately after umbilical lines
    (at 4 hours if not intubated and no lines)

  • If umbilical lines, nurse supine until at least 12 hours old
