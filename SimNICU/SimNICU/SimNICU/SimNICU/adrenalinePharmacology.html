Acts on alpha and beta receptors in the cardiovascular system. Use of adrenaline during bradycardia / cardiac arrest aims to cause a contraction of the heart muscle sufficient to move oxygenated blood to the coronary arteries and so reperfuse the heart. In hypotension high doses (>0.5 micrograms/kg/min) act mainly on alpha receptors to cause systemic vasoconstriction which may raise blood pressure. Lower doses (<0.5 micrograms/kg/min) act mainly on beta receptors, causing an increase in heart rate, stroke volume and contractility, along with systemic and pulmonary vasodilatation.
