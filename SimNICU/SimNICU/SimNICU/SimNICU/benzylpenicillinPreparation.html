1. Add 3.6ml water for injection to 600mg vial to give 600mg in 4ml.
     
  2. Draw up the dose. Can be further diluted in sodium chloride 0.9% or glucose 5% if neccesary.
     
  3. Give as a slow bolus, ideally over 5-10 minutes, to avoid CNS toxicity and convulsions. Ideally give 75mg/kg dose over 15-30 minutes.
     
  4. Flush with sodium chloride 0.9% or glucose 5%.
