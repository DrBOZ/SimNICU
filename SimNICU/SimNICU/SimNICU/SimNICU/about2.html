BACKGROUND

SimNICU started in 2011 as a project to develop a virtual neonatal unit for training and competency assessment.

This iPhone App was born to enable users to rapidly access information to help them through various on-line assessments.

Since then, I have focussed on improving the App to help tackle real world problems as well, and I sincerely hope that you find some of the tools and information contained within useful in your work.

I thank you very much for your support of SimNICU, which enables me to ensure continued development - I am working hard to add more features and usefulness.

Please feel free to contact me via Twitter: @SimNICU to suggest improvements or just to let me know where you are in the world!
