1. Cardiac Arrest:

    First dose:

    10 micrograms/kg (0.1ml/kg of 1 in 10,000 strength) IV.

    If there is likely to be delay establishing access then 20 micrograms/kg (0.2ml/kg of 1 in 10,000 strength) may be given via the ET tube.

    Subsequent doses:

    10-30 micrograms/kg
    (0.1 - 0.3 ml/kg of 1 in 10,000 strength) IV, or

    20 micrograms/kg (0.2 ml/kg of 1 in 10,000 strength) ET.

     
  2. Hypotension:

    0.1-0.3 micrograms/kg/min IV infusion (doses up to 1.5 micrograms/kg/minute can be used, but renal vasoconstriction may be a problem).
