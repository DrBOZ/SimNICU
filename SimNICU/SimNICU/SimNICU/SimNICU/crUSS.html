Indications

Any of the following:

  • < 1500g birth weight
  • < 33/40 gestation
  • All ventilated babies
  • Suspected congenital infection
    Symmetrical IUGR
    Thrombocytopenia (<50)
  • Seizures
    Encephalopathy
    Macrocephaly
    Meningitis
    Dysmorphic features
    Antenatal cranial abnormality
    Trauma

Timings of scans

  • Within 24 hours of birth
  • On day 3
  • Between 7 - 10 days old
  • On day 28 / Discharge

More scans will be needed if an abnormality is detected.
